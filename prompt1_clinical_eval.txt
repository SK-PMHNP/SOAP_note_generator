Clinical Evaluation Criteria:
- Accuracy: Note should not include any inaccurate data.
    If a section or data point was not discussed in the session, it should either be omitted from the note or include a note that this was not discussed.
- Objectivity: Assessments of patient and presenting problems should be clearly supported by data (no assumptions).
    Note should include relevant patient quotes to support subjective information.
    Note should avoid use of judgmental language.
- Thoroughness: All information from the session that a reasonable clinician would believe was necessary or important to document should be included in the note.
- Brevity: Note should remain as brief as possible while including all necessary information. Avoid repetition of content.
